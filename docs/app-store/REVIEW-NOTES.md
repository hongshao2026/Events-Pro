## English notes

Events Pro is a free tournament information and personal planning utility. It presents schedules for overseas poker festivals and lets users filter sessions, mark personal attendance or interest, view their calendar, and estimate a budget from published entry fees.

The app does not provide poker gameplay, betting, deposits, withdrawals, payments, or tournament registration. Selecting “参加” (Attend) changes only a local planning record; it does not reserve a seat or contact an organizer. Budget figures are informational estimates. The app is not affiliated with the listed organizers unless separately and explicitly documented.

No account or login is required. Schedules and the planner are bundled and available offline. Usernames, selections, preferences, custom exchange rates and local event changes remain on the device. There are no ads, analytics or tracking SDKs in this release. External official source links open in the system browser only when selected by the user.

To test:

1. Open “赛事” (Events), choose a festival, and open an event row.
2. In the detail footer choose “参加” (Attend) or “关注” (Watch).
3. Open “我的日程” (My Schedule) to view the selected session in the calendar.
4. Open “我的自选” (My Shortlist) to view the table and the local budget. The table can scroll horizontally.
5. Tap “导出图片” (Export Image) to generate the complete table locally. In the preview tap “分享或存储图片” (Share or Save Image) to open the iOS activity sheet. Canceling retains the preview and does not send anything automatically.
6. In “我的” (Profile), both planner and settings backups can be exported through the iOS activity sheet or restored from a selected JSON file.
7. Privacy, support and usage information are available under “隐私与支持” (Privacy and Support). Policies and help can be read in Simplified or Traditional Chinese. “清除本机记录” (Clear Local Records) asks for confirmation before removing the local planner and settings.

The primary interface is Simplified Chinese; original event names remain in English. Tournament times are shown in each festival's local time zone. The intended initial distribution is Taiwan only.
